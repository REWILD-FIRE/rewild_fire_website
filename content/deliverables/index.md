<h3>Expected deliverables</h3>

The project will produce several deliverables including land use maps, dataset on carbon sequestration and fire risk maps. Expected deliverables are:
- D1.1 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D1.1.pdf" "newtab" >}}Five-year maps and quantitative estimates of expected land cover under climate change and choice of rewilding strategy until 2060{{< /staticref >}}
- D1.2 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D1.2.pdf" "newtab" >}}Rewilding potential and priority regions at EU scale{{< /staticref >}}

- D2.1 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D2.1.pdf" "newtab" >}}Dataset on carbon sequestration following reforestation and proforestation{{< /staticref >}}
- D2.2 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D2.2.pdf" "newtab" >}}Dynamic models for reforestation and proforestation{{< /staticref >}}
  
- D3.1 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D3.1.pdf" "newtab" >}}Maps of burnt area and high fire risk zones at each replicated landscape{{< /staticref >}}
- D3.2 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D3.2.pdf" "newtab" >}}Estimates of carbon emissions by land use class within each eco-region to project carbon changes in environmental space and over time{{< /staticref >}}
  
- D4.1 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D4.1.pdf" "newtab" >}}Website, dissemination plan and graphic identity{{< /staticref >}}
- D4.2 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D4.2.pdf" "newtab" >}}Policy brief{{< /staticref >}}
  
- D5.1 : {{< staticref "uploads/deliverables/REWILDFIRE_D5.1_UNITO_20231110_0.2.pdf" "newtab" >}}Project guidebook{{< /staticref >}}
- D5.2 : Reports of the kick-off meeting and annual General assembly meetings (Internal documents)
